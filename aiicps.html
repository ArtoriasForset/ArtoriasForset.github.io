03 / CONNECTIVITY

Open connectivity. Industrial by design.

Use the right protocol for the right layer: field communication, device integration, telemetry and industrial information exchange.

OPC UA

Information modeling, structured device data and interoperable industrial services.

MQTT

Event-driven telemetry and publish/subscribe connectivity for distributed systems.

Modbus

Practical industrial data acquisition over TCP/IP and serial RTU networks.

REST / JSON

Lightweight northbound integration between industrial devices and software services.
